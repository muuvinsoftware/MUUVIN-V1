# MUUVIN V1 — 1.6.13

## 🎚️ Auto Level — every preset at the same volume

MUUVIN V1 now **matches the loudness of your presets automatically**. Instead of editing files in a bank, it's a real feature in the engine:

- The plugin **measures its own output** and applies a smooth makeup gain that lifts quiet presets up — and pulls loud ones down — toward a **shared target loudness**.
- It adapts **gently (no pumping)**, ignores silence and note gaps, and stays within a safe range (−6 … +12 dB) with the peak limiter still protecting the output.
- It works on **every preset** — the factory bank, your own sounds and any imported bank — with no per-file editing.

### How to use it

Open the **GLOBAL** tab and look for **AUTO LEVEL**. It is **on by default**, and your choice is saved. Turn it **off** to hear presets exactly as they were made.

Give a preset about a second of playing and it settles at the same level as the rest — browsing sounds no longer means reaching for the volume knob.

---

**Platform:** Windows (VST3, 64-bit).
**Install:** run `MUUVIN V1 Installer.exe` (or unzip the "VST3 plugin only" archive into your VST3 folder). Close your DAW before installing.
