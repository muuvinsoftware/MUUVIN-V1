# MUUVIN V1 — 1.6.14

## 🪟 Solid dropdown menus on the Glass theme

On the translucent **Glass** skin, the plugin's floating pop-up menus were drawing with a near-transparent background, so the knobs and controls behind them showed through and the menus were hard to read.

- The floating menus now use a **solid, opaque panel**: the FX **type / rate** selector, the **OSC A/B wavetable** menu, the **OSC C wavetable** menu and the **sampler** menu.
- Nothing bleeds through anymore — the lists read clearly over whatever is underneath, on every theme.

No sound or preset changes in this release — it's a UI fix only.

---

**Platform:** Windows (VST3, 64-bit).
**Install:** run `MUUVIN V1 Installer.exe` (or unzip the "VST3 plugin only" archive into your VST3 folder). Close your DAW before installing.
